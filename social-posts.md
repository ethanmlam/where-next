## LinkedIn Post

I built an interactive tracker mapping where 300 proven tech founders went after they left their companies.

5 eras. 355 sector switches. 11 sectors.

A few things stood out:

- Elon Musk: Fintech (PayPal) → Consumer (Tesla) → AI/Defense (xAI, SpaceX)
- Sam Altman: VC (Y Combinator) → AI (OpenAI)
- Reid Hoffman: Enterprise (LinkedIn) → VC (Greylock) → AI

The biggest signal? AI is the hottest destination by far — pulling founders from every sector.

This isn't a static chart. You can search any founder, toggle preset groups (PayPal Mafia, YC Mafia, Crypto OGs), or build your own custom view.

Check it out: esofast.com/where-next
